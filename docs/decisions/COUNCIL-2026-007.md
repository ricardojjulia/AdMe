# COUNCIL-2026-007: Real-Time Consumer Ad Transparency Inspector ("Why Am I Seeing This?"), Category Snooze Controls, and Privacy-Preserving Advertiser Quality Feedback Loop

**Status:** RATIFIED — Completed  
**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Council:** Architecture Council, AdMe (6/6 ratified)  
**Related:** COUNCIL-2026-001, COUNCIL-2026-004, COUNCIL-2026-005, COUNCIL-2026-006  
**Tags:** council, transparency, explainability, user-agency, relevance-tuning, quality-score, zero-knowledge  

## Context
In conventional digital ad ecosystems, ad targeting is opaque, unaccountable, and surveillance-driven. Users have no visibility into why particular commercial messages appear in their streams, creating suspicion, ad fatigue, and banner blindness. When ads are irrelevant, users feel helpless or annoyed because platforms offer little to no granular recourse beyond blunt ad blockers.

AdMe operates on Zero-Knowledge preference matching and client-side interest heuristics. However, prior to this decision:
1. **Zero Explainability**: Consumers could not see the explicit matching criteria (matching categories, proximity radius, auction context) behind any sponsored card.
2. **Coarse Feedback**: Users could only "Save" or "Report" an ad, lacking intermediate agency such as "Snooze this merchant for 30 days" or "See 50% less in this category".
3. **No Advertiser Quality Feedback Loop**: Advertisers in Ad Studio (`/studio`) could observe raw clicks and impressions, but had zero visibility into user relevance sentiment or negative friction metrics (snoozes, dismissals).

## Decision
Ratify an end-to-end Explainable Advertising & User Agency Architecture consisting of:

1. **Consumer Ad Transparency Inspector Modal (`TransparencyModal.tsx`)**:
   - Mounted via a discreet, accessible transparency chip (`ⓘ Why this ad?`) on all sponsored formats (`NativeAdCard`, `FeedCard`, `CarouselAdCard`).
   - Dynamically evaluates and reveals the exact client-side factors:
     * **Matched Interests**: Active user preferences intersecting with ad category tags.
     * **Proximity Context**: Neighborhood grid distance or edge-detected coarse metro baseline.
     * **Auction Context**: Current category auction placement without exposing proprietary bid amounts.
     * **Zero-Knowledge Privacy Attestation**: Explicit verification that matching occurred exclusively in browser RAM with zero PII transfer to the merchant.

2. **Immediate User Agency & Local Relevance Tuning**:
   - **"Snooze Merchant for 30 Days"**: Temporarily suppresses all ads from this merchant, persisted in `UserContext` and `localStorage`.
   - **"Down-weight Category"**: Attenuates category appearance frequency by -50% in the client-side heuristic feed scoring pipeline.
   - **"Not Relevant"**: Dismisses the card immediately with a smooth fade animation, re-ranking subsequent feed elements.

3. **Privacy-Preserving Relevance Feedback Endpoint (`/api/engagement/feedback`)**:
   - Transmits anonymous, un-fingerprinted feedback events (`action: 'snooze_advertiser' | 'downweight_category' | 'irrelevant' | 'helpful'`, `ad_id`, `category`) to Supabase.
   - Zero user IDs, zero IP addresses, and zero tracking cookies attached to the payload.

4. **Studio Ad Relevance & Health Indicator (`/studio`)**:
   - Displays real-time **Relevance & Quality Score** badge on each active campaign (e.g. `Relevance: 96% Excellent`), computing the ratio of positive views to negative feedback signals.
   - Empowers ethical advertisers to iterate on ad copy and targeting rather than spamming disengaged audiences.

## Amendments Adopted
1. **Pure Client-Side Decision Invariant**: The explainability logic must be computed client-side from the local `UserContext` and ad metadata, requiring zero additional network round-trips to open the Transparency Inspector.
2. **Cryptographic Zero-Knowledge Guarantee**: The modal must explicitly attest that no profile data was transmitted to the advertiser, referencing the local SHA-256 interest hash.
3. **Optimistic Local Feed Re-Ranking**: Snoozing an advertiser or category must immediately purge matching items from the in-memory feed array without requiring a full page refresh.
4. **Anonymized Feedback Egress**: Feedback signals sent to `/api/engagement/feedback` must be completely disassociated from user session tokens or accounts to preserve zero-knowledge anonymity.

## Implementation Mandate
1. Create `src/components/TransparencyModal.tsx` and `src/components/TransparencyModal.module.css`.
2. Integrate `TransparencyModal` and the `ⓘ Why this ad?` trigger into `src/components/NativeAdCard.tsx`, `src/components/FeedCard.tsx`, and `src/components/CarouselAdCard.tsx`.
3. Extend `UserContext.tsx` with `snoozedMerchants: string[]`, `categoryWeights: Record<string, number>`, `snoozeMerchant()`, and `adjustCategoryWeight()`.
4. Create the API route `src/app/api/engagement/feedback/route.ts` with strict rate-limiting and zero-telemetry sanitization.
5. Update `src/app/studio/page.tsx` to render the campaign Relevance & Quality Score badge.
6. Add unit tests in `src/components/transparency.test.ts` and verify with `npm run test`, `npm run typecheck`, and `npm run test:e2e`.

## Council Deliberations

### 🏛️ The Architect (YES)
This completes the architectural vision of AdMe as an explainable, consent-driven marketplace. Computing explainability factors entirely in client memory preserves our edge-first, zero-persistence principles. The feedback loop is decoupled: the client immediately mutates local feed state while optionally emitting an asynchronous, unauthenticated telemetry ping for aggregate analytics.

### ⚙️ The Engineer (YES)
Implementation is highly cohesive and clean. `TransparencyModal` mirrors the established pattern of `LeadModal` and `ReportModal`, utilizing React portals to prevent layout clipping. Extending `UserContext` with `snoozedMerchants` and `categoryWeights` integrates smoothly with our existing `performABSplitTest` and `heuristicScore` feed sorting routines.

### 🛡️ The Security Lead (YES)
From a privacy and regulatory perspective, this is a masterstroke. It directly complies with EU Digital Services Act (DSA) Article 26 requirements for advertising transparency and FTC guidance on deceptive algorithmic targeting. By ensuring the feedback endpoint accepts no user identifiers or authorization headers, we eliminate any risk of deanonymizing consumers through feedback patterns.

### 🎯 The Product Owner (YES)
This feature turns transparency into a competitive moat. When users see exactly why an ad appeared—and are given instant, one-click controls to snooze the brand or reduce the category—trust skyrockets. Users stop feeling hunted and start feeling in control. This will drastically reduce ad churn.

### 🧪 The QA Lead (YES)
The logic is deterministic and readily testable. We will write unit tests verifying that clicking "Snooze Merchant" excludes the merchant from subsequent feed filtering, that "Down-weight Category" updates category weights in localStorage, and that the transparency factors render the correct matched criteria.

### 💾 The Data Engineer (YES)
The feedback schema is simple and lightweight. In Supabase, feedback events can either map into the existing `engagements` table with `engagement_type IN ('snooze_advertiser', 'downweight_category', 'irrelevant')` or an anonymous summary view. No heavy database migrations or table locks required.
