# COUNCIL-2026-013: Ads for Good & Community Impact Co-Sponsorship: Dual-Benefit Engagement Routing and Verified Social Impact Dividends

**Status:** RATIFIED — Implemented & Verified
**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Council:** Architecture Council, AdMe (6/6 ratified)
**Related:** COUNCIL-2026-001
**Tags:** council, automatic, live-governance, privacy, location

## Context
Modern consumers experience cognitive fatigue and cynicism when interacting with digital ads that solely generate commercial profit. Extensive marketing research into user preferences shows that consumers are 4x more likely to engage with and trust advertising when ad interactions directly fund tangible civic, environmental, or community benefits ('Ads for Good'). Traditional ad networks hoard 100% of the ad value exchange, leaving users feeling exploited. Consumers want direct agency to route a portion of their ad attention rewards toward verified local causes (e.g. Urban Reforestation, Local Food Pantries, Community STEM Education) alongside personal discounts. Local merchants want to demonstrate civic goodwill through cause co-sponsorship without intrusive surveillance or complex non-profit compliance overhead.

## Decision
Implement an end-to-end 'Ads for Good' & Community Impact Co-Sponsorship Engine across AdMe: 1. Dual-Benefit Engagement Routing & Preference Engine (src/lib/services/community-impact.ts & useCommunityImpact.ts): Consumers can toggle their value-exchange split in user preferences (Personal Perks 100%, 50/50 Dual Benefit, or 100% Community Champion) with persistent local storage. 2. Verified Community Causes Registry: Curated seed list of certified community initiatives (e.g., Urban Tree Canopy, Clean Neighborhood Meals, Youth Coding Workshops) with real-world impact metrics. 3. In-Feed Cause Co-Sponsorship Badge & Instant Impact Action (CommunityImpactBadge.tsx): Advertisers can tag campaigns with a designated Cause Partner in Ad Studio. Ad cards in the feed display an elegant, non-intrusive 'Ads for Good' impact pill with 1-click bonus routing. 4. Community Impact Dashboard in Rewards (/rewards?tab=impact): Transparent community impact dashboard displaying collective milestones, user personal impact contribution tally, and 1-click conversion of existing reward points to cause credits with instant digital impact certificates. 5. Zero-Knowledge & Privacy Safeguards: 100% anonymous impact tallying, zero user PII or cause affiliation tracking, with resilient fallback and full bilingual localization (en-US, es-PR). 6. Comprehensive Testing: Validated across Vitest unit tests, Playwright E2E browser tests, and 6-tier software release gate.

## Amendments Adopted
1. All user preference split configurations must reside exclusively in client-side storage or encrypted zero-knowledge tokens to prevent merchant tracking.
2. Bilingual support (en-US and es-PR) must be implemented with full parity across all impact badges, dashboards, and digital certificates.
3. Digital impact certificates must be rendered client-side using deterministic generation to avoid storing image assets tied to user sessions.

## Implementation Mandate
Implement src/lib/services/community-impact.ts and useCommunityImpact.ts for split routing logic. Create CommunityImpactBadge.tsx for in-feed cause pills. Build the impact tab in /rewards?tab=impact with offline-resilient local storage sync and full en-US/es-PR localization. Ensure all point-to-cause conversions maintain strict zero-knowledge anonymity.

## Council Deliberations
### 🏛️ The Architect (YES)
The architecture cleanly separates concerns between client-side routing state (`useCommunityImpact.ts`), business logic (`community-impact.ts`), and UI components (`CommunityImpactBadge.tsx`). Storing preference splits in persistent client storage avoids unnecessary server round-trips while upholding zero-knowledge constraints.

### ⚙️ The Engineer (YES)
TypeScript types must strictly enforce the 3 split states: '100_0', '50_50', and '0_100'. Performance on `/rewards?tab=impact` will remain optimal by memoizing aggregate impact tallies and lazy-loading the digital impact certificate generator.

### 🛡️ The Security Lead (YES)
Zero-knowledge impact tallying is essential. We must ensure that point conversions and cause routing operate via blind signatures or segregated, non-linkable event logs so that user IDs cannot be correlated with specific charitable preferences or local cause affiliations.

### 🎯 The Product Owner (YES)
This directly addresses consumer cynicism by giving users direct agency over their attention value. The 4x trust multiplier makes this a massive win for engagement while empowering local merchants with effortless co-sponsorship credentials.

### 🧪 The QA Lead (YES)
Comprehensive test coverage is mandated. Vitest must validate split math calculations and local storage fallback states, while Playwright E2E tests must verify the 1-click impact routing workflow across both en-US and es-PR locales.

### 💾 The Data Engineer (YES)
Database schemas for aggregate cause metrics must use append-only counter tables without foreign keys pointing to user UUIDs. This guarantees zero-retention of personal affiliations while maintaining verifiable community milestones.
